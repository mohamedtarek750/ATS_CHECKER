Data Analyst — Administrative Capital for Urban Development
Location: New Administrative Capital, Egypt. Full time, on site.

ABOUT THE ROLE
We are looking for a Data Analyst to join the Digital Services team. You will own
the reporting layer used by the operations and commercial teams, and turn messy
operational data into something people can actually make decisions from.

REQUIREMENTS
- Bachelor degree in Statistics, Computer Science, Engineering or a related field.
- Minimum 2 years of professional experience in a data or reporting role.
- Strong SQL. You must be comfortable with joins, window functions and tuning a
  slow query.
- Experience building dashboards in Power BI or Tableau is required.
- Working knowledge of Excel, including Power Query.
- Good written English.

PREFERRED
- Python for data work (pandas) is a plus.
- Exposure to cloud data platforms such as Azure or Databricks is desirable.
- Experience in retail, logistics or public sector reporting would be an advantage.
- A relevant certification (PL-300, Google Data Analytics) is nice to have.

WHAT WE OFFER
Competitive salary, medical insurance, transport allowance, and a training budget.
